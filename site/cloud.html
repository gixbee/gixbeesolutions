Platform

Hosted on Microsoft Azure.

The apps stay useful only while the service is up. Sign-in, live jobs, photos, and alerts all run in the cloud. Gixbee hosts that service on Microsoft Azure.

Cloud services

What the product depends on.

These are the services behind the apps. They grow with jobs in Kerala first, then the rest of India, then the world.

ServiceWhat it is for
ComputeThe API the Android and iOS apps call, including live updates during a job.
DatabaseAccounts, bookings, businesses, jobs, and rentals.
CacheSign-in codes, who is available to work, and background tasks.
StorageProfile photos and files.
NetworkingMaps, alerts, and media while a job is underway.
MonitoringWhether the live service is healthy.

Always on

A professional can go live at any hour. Compute, database, and cache stay available for that.

During a job

An active job adds live updates, alerts, and media. Networking and storage follow the number of jobs.

Across the market

Usage starts in Kerala, then the rest of India, then the world. The same six services carry that load.
