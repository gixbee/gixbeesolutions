Terms

Using Gixbee.

These terms cover the Gixbee website and apps. Last updated 19 October 2026.

The service

Gixbee is software for booking local services, planning events, finding work, and listing a business. The focus is Kerala first, then the rest of India, then the world. Gixbee does not employ the professionals on the platform, and it does not assign a job to someone who has not accepted it. A request may go unanswered if nobody accepts.

Accounts and listings

You sign in with your phone number. You are responsible for that account and for what you publish in a profile, listing, or job. Listings must describe a real service.

Money

The customer pays the professional directly for the job, then both confirm it in the app. Fees for using Gixbee are shown in the app before you pay them.
