Terms

Using Gixbee.

These terms cover this website and the Gixbee apps. Last updated 19 October 2026.

The service

Gixbee helps people book local services, plan events, find jobs, take gig work, and list a business in Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Gixbee facilitates that. It does not employ the pros on the platform, and it does not assign a job to someone who has not accepted it.

Accounts

You sign in with your phone number. You are responsible for the activity on that account and for the details you publish in a profile, listing, or job post.

Bookings and hiring

A booking or a hire exists when the people involved take the steps the app asks for. Availability depends on who is live and who accepts. Gixbee does not promise that a request will be accepted.

Cancellation follows the rules shown in the app. Cancelling is free while a request is still searching and for a short period after accept. Later cancels can require the pro’s agreement. Repeated late cancels can pause Instant Help until travel compensation is paid.

Money

The job amount is paid by the customer to the pro, in cash or UPI, and both confirm it in the app. Charges Gixbee itself makes — job credits, the working-period fee, hiring match fees, and late-cancel travel compensation — are taken through Razorpay at the prices shown before you pay.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the service: false identity, harassment, interfering with another booking, or attempting to break into accounts or the API. Business listings must describe a real service.

The website

This site describes the product. Features in the apps are the ones you see after you sign in. If a screen and this page disagree, the screen is the current behavior.
