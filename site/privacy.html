Privacy

What the app uses.

This note covers the Gixbee apps. It was last updated on 19 October 2026. Gixbee starts in Kerala, then the rest of India. Contact gixbeesolutions@gmail.com.

Information

  • Phone number, to sign in and to reach you about a job.
  • Name, skills, and the profile or business details you choose to publish.
  • Location while you book, go live, or follow a trip. People on that job can see the trip.
  • Messages and records of bookings, rentals, and hiring that you create in the app.
  • A token so your phone can receive alerts.

Identity and payments

Professionals complete an identity check before going live. Gixbee does not keep the full identity number. Fees charged inside the app are handled by the payment provider. Gixbee does not store card numbers.

How it is used

We use this information to run the apps: sign-in, bookings, live trips, alerts, and support. Gixbee does not sell personal information.

Sign-in codes are sent by a text-message provider and expire quickly. Account and booking records stay while the account is in use, and as needed to finish a job or meet a legal duty. Write to gixbeesolutions@gmail.com to ask about your account.

Gixbee is for adults arranging services, work, and hiring.
