Privacy

What the app uses.

This note covers the Gixbee mobile apps and the staff console. It was last updated on 19 October 2026.

Who we are

Gixbee is the Skill Intelligence Network, a service for local bookings, gig work, jobs, and business listings in Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Support: gixbeesolutions@gmail.com.

Information the product uses

  • Phone number, for the one-time password login and for job contact.
  • Name and profile details you enter, including skills, a talent profile, business listings, and saved addresses.
  • Location, when you book, go live as a pro, or follow a trip. Live location on a job is shared with the people on that job.
  • Booking, rental, message, and hiring records created while you use the app.
  • A push token so the phone can receive alerts when the app is in the background.
  • Support messages and, if the app crashes, a crash report sent to Gixbee.
  • Payment references for charges Gixbee itself makes through Razorpay. Card and UPI details are collected by Razorpay, not stored as card numbers in Gixbee.

Identity checks

Pros verify with Aadhaar OTP through a KYC provider. Gixbee does not store the full Aadhaar number. It keeps a verification status.

Why

We use this information to sign you in, match and run bookings, show a live trip, deliver messages and alerts, take the fees described in the product, review businesses, and answer support. Staff with access to the console use it to handle verification, disputes, and support.

Other processors

  • SMS provider, for login codes.
  • Razorpay, for Gixbee’s own charges.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, for push alerts.
  • A KYC provider, for Aadhaar OTP verification.
  • Map tiles, for the live trip map.
  • The cloud host that runs the API, database, cache, and file storage.

What we do not do

Gixbee does not sell personal information. The live-job status screen does not publish personal phone numbers for the in-app call. Ordinary app responses are not a dump of private account fields.

How long

Account, booking, and hiring records stay while the account is in use and as needed to finish a job, handle a dispute, or meet a legal duty. Login codes are short-lived. To ask about your account, email gixbeesolutions@gmail.com.

Children

Gixbee is for adults arranging services, work, and hiring. It is not directed at children.
