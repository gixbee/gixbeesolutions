Gixbee facilitates. It does not assign the work. Every booking, acceptance, and hire is a choice by the people involved.

Four intents

One login. Four ways to use it.

After a phone login, the home screen opens the path the person needs that day. The account is not locked to a single role.

01

Book services

Instant Help for an electrician, plumber, driver, cleaner, nurse, carpenter, painter, or cook. Or plan an event: hall, catering, decoration, photography, and rentals.

02

Find a job

Build a profile, browse the job board, track applications, and respond to Smart Hiring offers from businesses that are hiring.

03

Earn by working

Register skills, verify identity, go live, and accept nearby gig requests. Pay for job credits and the working period through the app.

04

List a business

Register a service, hiring, or rental business. Run hiring, manage a venue or rental catalog, and keep a calendar of days you are offline.

Instant Help

Request a skill. A live pro accepts.

01

Ask for the work

The customer names the skill and the place. Gixbee offers the request to pros who are live nearby. The customer does not pick a person from a list.

02

Follow the trip

After a pro accepts, the customer sees live location on a map and can place an in-app voice call. Phone numbers stay off that status screen.

03

Pay the pro directly

The job itself is settled between customer and pro, in cash or UPI, and both confirm it in the app. Instant Help also adds a one-way travel amount from the distance at accept.

Trust

Identity, calls, and cancellations are explicit.

Pro verification

Pros verify with Aadhaar OTP. Gixbee keeps a verification status. It does not store the full Aadhaar number.

In-app voice

The call button on a live job opens an in-app call. The booking status does not hand out personal phone numbers.

Cancel rules

Cancelling is free while Gixbee is still searching, and for a short grace period after a pro accepts. After arrival, the pro has to agree. Repeated late cancels can pause Instant Help until the pro’s travel is covered.
