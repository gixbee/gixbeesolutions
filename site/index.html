The Skill Intelligence Network.

Gixbee is a software product for local services and work. People use the Android and iOS apps to book help, plan an event, find a job, or list a business. One account can do any of those.

Gixbee builds the software. It does not employ the professionals, and it does not assign the work. A booking happens when someone accepts it.

The product

Four ways to use one app.

After a phone sign-in, the home screen opens the path the person needs. The account is not locked to a single role.

01

Book help

Request an electrician, plumber, driver, cleaner, nurse, carpenter, painter, or cook. Or plan a hall, catering, decoration, photography, and rentals.

02

Find a job

Create a profile, browse jobs, and respond when a business is hiring for the kind of work you do.

03

Earn

Register your skills, complete an identity check, go live, and accept nearby requests when you want them.

04

List a business

Register a service, hiring, or rental business and manage the work from the same app.

A booking

Request a skill. Someone accepts.

01

Ask

The customer names the skill and the place. Gixbee offers the request to professionals who are live nearby.

02

Follow

After someone accepts, the customer follows the trip in the app and can call inside the job.

03

Pay the professional

The customer pays the professional directly. Both confirm it in the app. Fees for using Gixbee are charged separately in the app.
