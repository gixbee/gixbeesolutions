Product

Software the company owns.

Gixbee is the product. It ships as Android and iOS apps. Households, professionals, and local businesses use it themselves. Gixbee is not a staffing agency and not a consultancy.

Book

Local help and events

Instant help covers electrician, plumber, driver, cleaner, nurse, carpenter, painter, and cook. The customer requests the skill. A professional who is live can accept. The trip stays visible in the app.

Plan an event covers hall, catering, decoration, photography, and equipment or space rentals, with dates chosen in the app.

Work

Jobs, hiring, and going live

People looking for work keep a profile and browse jobs. Businesses list a service, a hiring need, or rentals. Hiring matches people who have said they do that work.

A professional registers skills, passes an identity check, and goes live only when they want requests. Gixbee does not push a job onto someone who has not accepted it.

Money

Two different payments.

The job amount is paid by the customer to the professional, then confirmed in the app. Charges for using Gixbee, such as going live or a hiring match, are paid in the app.
