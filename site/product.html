Product

A working app, not a brochure.

Gixbee ships as Android and iOS apps plus a staff console. The notes below describe what those surfaces do today.

Book

Instant Help

Skills on the home screen: electrician, plumber, driver, cleaner, nurse, carpenter, painter, and cook. The request goes to pros who are live. The customer watches the trip, talks in the app, and settles the job with the pro.

  • Live map after accept
  • In-app voice call
  • Cash or UPI, confirmed by both sides
  • One-way travel amount on Instant Help, from the distance at accept
  • Free cancel while searching, then a short grace period
Plan

Events and rentals

Plan an Event covers hall, catering, decoration, and photography. Rentals are a catalog: browse an item, pick a date and time, and reserve it. Owners manage their own listings and reservations.

  • Calendar selection with overlap blocking on rentals
  • Venue owners manage halls, enquiries, and quotes
  • Service businesses can mark days offline
Work

Earn by working

A pro registers skills, completes Aadhaar OTP verification, and goes live when they want jobs. Incoming work is a queue they accept. Gixbee does not push a job onto someone who has not accepted it.

  • Go Live is gated on verification, location, and credits
  • Job-credit packs and a period fee are paid in the app through Razorpay
  • The customer’s job payment stays between customer and pro
Hire

Find a job and hire

Job seekers keep a profile, browse a job board, and track applications. Smart Hiring is the matching path: seekers save job types, businesses start a hire, and seekers mark interested or not interested.

  • Offers move through pending, shortlisted, rejected, and hired
  • A hiring business has to be verified before it starts a match
  • Match fees are charged in the app through Razorpay
  • Push alerts for offers and status changes

Payments inside the app

What Gixbee charges, and what it does not.

The job amount is paid by the customer to the pro, outside Gixbee’s balance, and both people confirm it. Gixbee uses Razorpay for its own charges: job-credit packs, the pro period fee, hiring match fees, and late-cancel travel compensation.

There is no in-app wallet on the live product. Card and UPI details for those Razorpay charges are handled by Razorpay.
